### Enhanced Concept:  
**Augmented Reality Dining Experience: "Taste the Future" – A Culinary Odyssey**

### Revamped Unique Features:
1. **Immersive Interactive Menus**:
   - **Enhanced AR Interface**: Guests don’t just view 3D models; they can interact with them! Swiping through ingredients reveals their nutritional information, environmental impact, and chef anecdotes. Think of it as an augmented experience that marries food with storytelling.

2. **Personalized Flavor Journeys**:
   - **Taste Profiling**: Before dining, diners complete a quick flavor profile quiz through the AR app. Based on their responses, the app curates a tailored menu experience, suggesting custom dishes and drink pairings that cater specifically to their palates.

3. **Live Chef Connection**:
   - **Interactive Chef Huddles**: Instead of just a stream, create scheduled “Chef Huddles” where guests can join live discussions about culinary techniques, ingredient sourcing, and even participate in live voting for future dishes.

4. **Themed AR Experiences**:
   - **Cultural Journeys**: Each dining session can be themed around different cuisines (e.g., Italian Night, Asian Fusion), with AR experiences that transport diners to the origin of the dishes. Imagine being able to see a beautiful Italian vineyard or bustling Tokyo market right from your table!

5. **Collaborative Cooking**:
   - **AR Recipe Cards**: Guests can receive AR cards that guide them through cooking their selected dish at home post-experience, encouraging them to recreate it with a personal twist. This creates an ongoing relationship with your brand long after they've left!

### Enhanced Marketing Strategy:
- **Pixelated Hints Campaign**: Launch a visually stunning teaser campaign where you release pixelated images of dishes that slowly reveal themselves over time. Use countdowns and AR filters on social media to engage audiences and build anticipation.

- **Influencer Experience Days**: Beyond partnerships, host exclusive influencer nights where they can live-stream their AR dining experiences. Create a bespoke hashtag for their followers to engage with, encouraging user-generated content.

- **Early Access VIP Program**: Allow loyal customers to join an exclusive early-access list that offers sneak peeks or even participation in menu curation, giving them a stake in the experience and enhancing their emotional connection to your brand.

- **Interactive Feedback Loop**:
   - **AR Story Walls**: After dining, guests can leave their feedback in an AR story wall that other diners can interact with, creating a communal experience. This also serves as engaging content for marketing materials!

### Event Tie-Ins:
- Consider tying this experience to local events or festivals to maximize visibility. Partner with local artists to create AR art installations that guests can experience while waiting for a table.
